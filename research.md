# Research Documentation

## Interview with Ashlee Wang

Conducted via video call. Three structured questions, asked in Chinese (Ashlee's preferred language).

**问题1：在使用电脑作业时，是不是在寻找/想要applied文件时感觉麻烦？**
(Question 1: When working on assignments on your computer, do you find it troublesome to locate or access the files you need?)
- 是的。("Yes.")

**问题2：在你的日常生活中，你的开始写作业到完成作业保存的流程是什么？**
(Question 2: In your daily life, what is your workflow from starting an assignment to saving the finished work?)
1. 打开提交作业的网站查找作业要求 (Open the submission site to check assignment requirements)
2. 打开浏览器开始搜索资料 (Open the browser to search for reference material)
3. 打开空白文档开始完成 brainstorm (Open a blank document to brainstorm)
4. 写 final draft (Write the final draft)

**问题3：当你寻找之前完成的作业时，你的步骤是什么？**
(Question 3: When you need to find a previously completed assignment, what are your steps?)
1. 打开电脑的搜索器，搜索关键词或者记得的文档名字 (Open the system search tool, search by keyword or whatever filename I can remember)
2. 如果电脑没有存档，去 Google Docs 查找 (If it's not saved on my computer, go to Google Docs to look for it)

## Observed Pain Points

- No organizational step exists in her workflow — files are created and abandoned in place
- Relies on memory (filenames, keywords) to retrieve past work
- Splits files between local desktop and Google Drive with no bridge between them
- Has no course-based mental model for her file system, even though courses are the primary context for all her academic work
